Who I Am

I was born in San Antonio, Texas but I have lived in Arizona for most of my life. Growing up I always had a passion for creativity and making art. Throughout school, I was always taking art classes whether that be drawing, painting, or ceramics. I even had a brief stint in dance and drama before realizing that my true passion lies in design. When I went to college I discovered I have a passion for photography. My grandmother gave me one of her old film cameras and since then I have never not had a camera on me. My dad and I share this love and it had become a part of who I am. My interest in photography, and the classes I have taken for it, have taught me so much about design and I carry these skills with me into my professional pursuits.
